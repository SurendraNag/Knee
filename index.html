Anatomy Explainer
Educational demo, not for diagnosis

Show your patient what happened, in 3D

Pick an injury or a body part, turn the model to the best view, then send your patient a view-only link.

    Body parts

    How it works

    1. Pick. Tap a common injury, or open a body part and tap Injury.
    2. Show. Turn and zoom the model. Use Display for see-through bone, labels and layers.
    3. Send. Share gives a view-only link for your patient. Nothing is stored on a server and the link holds no patient details.

Cartilage, many ligaments and some nerves are modelled. Injuries outside the knee are a preview, waiting for a doctor to review them.
