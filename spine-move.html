⌂ Spine Movement
Educational demo, not for diagnosis
Moves:

Each vertebra moves as a solid bone; the discs between them squeeze on one side and open on the other. How much each level moves follows typical adult ranges.

Tap a disc on the model, or pick one above. Then drag the disc with your finger or mouse to push it out.
